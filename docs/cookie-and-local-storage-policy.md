## 1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Tsumugi may use cookies, local storage, session storage, and similar browser technologies. It supplements the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

## 2. Current planning position

The current landing page is a static evaluation page. It does not intentionally require account cookies, analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or local-storage persistence for its core presentation. This statement must be rechecked before publication and after authentication or analytics are implemented.

## 3. Categories

The final policy should list each technology by name, provider, purpose, duration, and whether it is first-party or third-party.

### Essential technologies

These may be needed for security, session management, authentication, load balancing, accessibility preferences, or other functions explicitly requested by the user. Where legally required, they should be limited to what is strictly necessary.

### Preference technologies

These may remember choices such as display or navigation preferences. The final product must state whether consent is required and how a person can change or clear the preference.

### Analytics and measurement

These may help Moeko Works understand performance and usage. They must not be described as active unless an analytics provider and configuration are actually deployed. The final version should explain consent, opt-out, retention, and provider details.

### Advertising or tracking technologies

No advertising or cross-site tracking technology is planned for the current landing page. If that changes, this policy and any consent experience must be updated before deployment.

## 4. Local storage and session storage

Browser storage may be used for temporary interface state or user preferences. Storage values may remain until they expire, are cleared by the service, or are removed by the user through browser controls. The final policy should list each key or key category that is actually used and whether it contains personal information.

## 5. Managing choices

Users can usually delete cookies and browser storage through browser settings. Blocking essential technologies may prevent account or security features from working. If non-essential technologies are deployed, Tsumugi should provide a consent or preference control where required and honor the selected choices.

## 6. Third parties

Third-party providers may place or access browser technologies only if the deployed service uses them. The final policy should identify those providers and link to their privacy information.

## 7. Updates

Moeko Works may update this policy when browser technologies or legal requirements change. The final version should show the latest effective date and explain material changes.
