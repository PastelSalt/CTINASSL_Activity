## 1. Who this notice applies to

This Privacy Policy explains how Moeko Works would handle personal information when a person visits the Tsumugi website, creates or uses a Tsumugi account, or contacts the project team.

## 2. Information we may collect

The final policy should describe only information the service actually collects. The current planning categories are:

- **Account information:** name, username, email address, authentication identifiers, and account preferences.
- **Workspace information:** project names, objectives, requirements, milestones, tasks, assets, revisions, documentation, and deliverables entered by users.
- **Technical information:** IP address, browser and device type, operating system, approximate location derived from IP, referral information, and logs needed to secure and operate the service.
- **Communications:** messages, support requests, feedback, and information provided when contacting Moeko Works.
- **Preference storage:** cookie or local-storage values described in the [Cookie & Local Storage Policy](./cookie-and-local-storage-policy.md).

## 3. How we may use information

Subject to the final product design and applicable law, information may be used to:

1. provide, maintain, and secure Tsumugi;
2. create and manage accounts and workspaces;
3. save, organize, and display project records at the user's request;
4. respond to support requests and service communications;
5. diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and improve reliability;
6. comply with legal obligations and enforce the service terms; and
7. provide analytics or optional features only when those practices are actually enabled and disclosed.

## 4. Legal bases

Where applicable, the final policy should identify the legal bases relied upon for each processing purpose, such as contract performance, legitimate interests, consent, or legal obligation. Moeko Works must complete this section for the jurisdictions in which Tsumugi is offered.

## 5. When information may be shared

Moeko Works should not sell personal information. Information may be shared with:

- infrastructure, hosting, storage, email, authentication, security, and support providers that process information for the service;
- professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement, or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety; and
- a successor involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate notice where required.

The final version must list material providers or link to an up-to-date category list where required.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We plan to retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this notice, to provide the service, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations. The final policy should state retention periods or practical criteria for account data, workspace content, security logs, backups, and support records.

Users should be told how to request account deletion and what may remain temporarily in backups or records required by law.

## 7. Security

Moeko Works plans to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No online service can guarantee absolute security. The final policy should identify any material security practices that can be accurately disclosed.
